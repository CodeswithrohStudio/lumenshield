# Reference Board

Created: 2026-08-14
Mode: Persuade + Operate
Design read: landing + app shell for cautious DeFi users and hackathon judges, premium dark financial cockpit lane.
Dials: variance 7, motion 5, density 6, art direction 7

## Quality Bar

- Yoldr live product: strong emotional landing interaction and direct consumer copy. Borrow the immediate vault metaphor, not Flow-specific implementation language.
- Flare developer docs: implementation credibility, exact network details, FAssets/FXRP, FTSO, and FDC vocabulary.
- Professional DeFi dashboards: clear positions, balances, network evidence, and action review states.

## Borrow

- Palette/material: deep dark cockpit surfaces with one Flare-orange signal accent and a colder blue trust/action layer.
- Type/hierarchy: confident geometric display for public promise, highly readable sans for app operation.
- Layout/composition: product workbench landing, with app/evidence visual above the fold.
- Motion/interaction: one hero reveal, one signal-line or evidence reveal, restrained app state transitions.
- Asset language: code-native vault diagrams, contract evidence panels, real transaction/status rows.

## Avoid

- Generic DeFi neon gradients.
- "Risk-free" copy.
- Security shield cliché as the whole identity.
- Fake browser chrome.
- Invented metrics, fake partners, fake audits.
- Flare as a badge instead of core product logic.

## Direction Contract

- Thesis: principal stays shielded, yield follows the signal.
- First viewport: split promise and product/evidence mockup; Flare proof visible early.
- System: dark premium panels, generated premium palette, Unbounded/Albert Sans, compact app shell.
- Risk: overclaiming safety, building a visually nice shell without enough Flare-native evidence.
